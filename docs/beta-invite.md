# Teacher Beta Invite

Two pieces of copy for the TestFlight rollout. Keep them in sync — if the focus
of the build changes, update both.

---

## 1. TestFlight "What to Test" field

Paste this into App Store Connect → TestFlight → your build → **What to Test**.
TestFlight surfaces it to testers inside the TestFlight app before they install.

```
Thanks for testing Creator — a choreography tool built for dance teachers.

What this build covers
- Create a piece, add your dancers, lay them out on the stage
- Build multiple formations and preview transitions between them
- Export a PDF or PNG of your formations to share or print
- Save your work to your device (cloud sync in progress)

What we need from you
- Use it like you would for real rehearsal. Make a piece. Build it out.
  Tell us where it breaks.
- Tap the bug icon in the bottom right corner to report anything weird.
  Screenshots help a ton.
- Notice anything that feels slow, confusing, or just wrong — that's the
  feedback we want most.

Known gaps
- Iconography and onboarding are still getting polished
- A few features you might expect (like offline mode, multi-user sharing)
  aren't built yet

Reach Dixon directly at dancewithdixon@gmail.com.
```

---

## 2. Personal email template

Send alongside the TestFlight invite link. Edit the opener for each teacher.

```
Hey {{first name}},

Quick ask — I've been building a choreography tool (Creator) for dance
teachers, and I'd love your eyes on it before it goes wider. You're on my
short list of people I trust to tell me when something is half-baked.

TestFlight invite is here: {{TestFlight link}}

What I'm hoping you'll do:
1. Install it on your iPad.
2. Use it for ONE real piece you're working on right now — 10-15 minutes
   of real use beats an hour of clicking around.
3. When you find something broken, slow, or confusing, tap the little bug
   icon at the bottom right of the app and send it through. Screenshots
   and a sentence of context are gold.

I'm not asking for long feedback emails. Just use it, note the friction,
hit the bug button. I'll iterate fast.

This is free while in beta — no pressure, no expectations. If it's useful,
great. If it's not for you, also totally fine.

Thanks for looking at this.

Dixon
```

## Notes for Dixon

- The bug report button is already live — it's the floating "Report Bug"
  button at the bottom right. It captures screenshots, device info, and
  ships straight to the admin view in Settings (visible only to your
  account since you're the admin email).
- Review bug reports in-app: Settings → Bug Reports section (admin only).
- If a teacher says "I can't find the bug button" — it means they got
  logged out or hit the wrong screen. First thing to verify.
